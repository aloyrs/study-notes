Stack – std::stack (header <stack>): LIFO structure. Key methods: push, pop, top, empty, size.

Queue – std::queue (header <queue>): FIFO structure. Key methods: push, pop, front, back, empty, size.

Priority Queue – std::priority_queue (header <queue>): Heap-based queue (max-heap by default). Key methods: push, pop, top, empty, size.

Linked List – std::list (header <list>): Doubly linked list. Key methods: push_front, push_back, pop_front, pop_back, insert, erase, begin, end.
